The word 耽翫 (/taŋɡaɴ/) represents the feeling of being so wholly consumed by a beautiful thing that the world outside it ceases to exist.
It is the scholar who meant to read a single page and surfaces, blinking, to find the candle burned to nothing and the night already old.
It is the listener dissolved into music, seeing in the sound waves the vivid colors of her life.

You will find here my ramblings about things I find interesting ; mostly language learning, IT, and the future.
Feel free to poke around. Nothing bites. Probably.
