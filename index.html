耽翫(たんがん)：その事ばかりが好きになり熱中する。熱中して研究する。

// learning is a lifelong endeavour

The word 耽翫 (/taŋɡaɴ/) represents the feeling of being so wholly consumed by a beautiful thing that the world outside of it ceases to exist.
It is the scholar who meant to read a single page and surfaces, blinking, to find the candle burned to nothing and the night already old.
It is the composer dissolved into music, seeing in the sound waves the vivid colors of their life.

You will find here my ramblings about things I find interesting ; mostly language learning, computers, and progress.

about me

I have spent my life so far learning how to learn.
Some of my inspirations are Khatzumoto (creator of AJATT), Piotr Woźniak (creator of Supermemo), and Mike Israetel (famous Youtuber).

My main pleasure in life is reading texts in classical Chinese, and learning new words like a hoarder.

I used to spend a great ammount of thought in my choices of technology ; but I have since found a computing environment that suits me, so I've stopped caring about that so much.

If you want to contact me, I often hang around this Discord server.
