English.

This website is written in English.

The whole world works with English.

I sometimes wonder why I spend so much time learning any language other than English.

Of course, I only learn languages for fun as a hobby, it's not like I'm suffering through it, thankfully.

But then comes this question, that I do not have an answer to : for how long will humanity be able to enjoy the aftermath of the tower of Babel ?

As the old story counts, humanity got punished by God for trying to approach the heavens. The punishment would be a curse, splitting the then universal language of humanity into the variety of tongues and dialects seen nowadays.

It definitely feels like as society progresses, as globalisation advances, we are working our way out of this Babel curse. Don't you think ?
