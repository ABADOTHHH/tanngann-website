Why I Switched to Neovim

2025-09-15  //  dev

I used VS Code for years. It's fine. It does everything, runs everywhere, and requires almost no configuration. That's also its problem: it does everything, and I only need some of it.

Neovim with a lean config is faster, stays out of the way, and keeps me in the terminal where most of my work already lives. The modal editing model, once internalized, is hard to give up.

The Lua config ecosystem has matured to the point where you can get LSP, treesitter, fuzzy finding, and Git integration without fighting your tools. It just takes a weekend to set up and a month to stop reaching for the mouse.
