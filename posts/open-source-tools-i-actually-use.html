Open Source Tools I Actually Use

2025-06-12  //  misc

Not a comprehensive list. Not a "top 10 tools every hacker needs" article. Just the things I actually open on a regular basis and would miss if they disappeared.

ripgrep for searching code. fzf for finding files. tmux for staying sane in the terminal. Obsidian for notes, even though it's not strictly open source. Ghidra when I need a disassembler and don't want to pay for IDA.

The common thread: they do one thing well, they're fast, and they compose with other tools rather than trying to replace them. The Unix philosophy, more or less, still holds up.
