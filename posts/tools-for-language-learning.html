The language learning community has been extremely prolific, comming with a myriad of software useful to language learners.

We are extremely fortunate to live in this time, I tell you !
Many thanks to all the people who have participated in fostering such a rich and convenient world for language learners.

Yomitan : Unlocks your 写輪眼, makes you able to understand most of what you'd want to read from day one.

Anki : My memory insurance.

HakuNeko : Download manager with various manga and anime sources preset.

Manatan : Complete media library with OCR for manga and anime.

GameSentenceMiner : Quite complex immmersion suite. I only use it for its video OCR functionality, for videos with hard subs.

Audacity : The humble and almighty audio editor ; I do all my audio capture and edits with this trusty software.

If you think of anything else that could be interesting to add here, feel free to open an issue on Github.
