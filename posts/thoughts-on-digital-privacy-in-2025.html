Thoughts on Digital Privacy in 2025

2025-08-18  //  thoughts

Privacy isn't a product you buy or an app you install. It's a practice — a series of deliberate choices about what you share, with whom, and under what conditions.

The default in 2025 is still surveillance. Most services are free because you are the asset. Understanding that changes how you evaluate tools, but it doesn't mean opting out of everything is realistic or even desirable.

The pragmatic approach: reduce your surface area where it matters to you, accept the tradeoffs where it doesn't, and stay clear-eyed about what you're actually protecting against. Threat models exist for a reason.
