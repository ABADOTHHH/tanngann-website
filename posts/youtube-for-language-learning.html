The hardest part in learning a language, is building a strong immersion environment.

You can find here some Youtube channels in Chinese and Japanese, to get you started.
You won't like all of them, you might find none of them interesting.
Anyway, now you have to go out there, and find more material that fits your particular interests.
Think of things that you like to read or watch in the languages you already use, and try to go find an equivalent in your target language.
The cool thing with Youtube is that the more you'll show interest for videos in your target language, the more you'll get recommended stuff in your target language.

Japanese

Gaming

牛沢 : I like his voice. Check out his playlists on 龍が如く０, ゼルダBOTW, and ヘビの命.

キヨ : Solo gaming with a really lively persona. Check out his playlists on 逆転裁判, ゼルダ, and マリオパーティ.

Culture/Education

中田敦彦 : The biggest educational channel on japanese YT. Very good to enhance your cultural understanding on 日本史 and 現代社会.

大人の教養TV : Education videos with hard subs.

QuizKnock : Team of quiz enthusiasts, they have some videos with very rare vocab.

マイティー・ポー : Chinese dude praising stuff that he finds cool about japan. Check out his old videos, they were cool.

むいむい : Chinese girl talking about cultural differences between japan and china.

李姉妹ch : Chinese girls talking about cultural differences between japan and china.

シンノスケ : Japanese dude in Kenya.

Traditional east asian games

クリアレインのアトリエ : Check out his 初心者向け playlist to learn how to play 麻雀.

飛田早紀 : Commented 囲碁 gameplay.

Entertainment

れてんジャダム : My favorite youtube channel. I think their humour is either hit or miss, but holy cow some of their videos are absolute gems. Check out their collab videos with ゼパ and 芳賀セブン.

じゃだれてセンチメンタル : れてんジャダム have their main channel demonetized, so now they upload here.

芳賀セブン : Bodybuilder with fun persona. His best videos are the ones made featuring れてんジャダム.

ゼパ : Girl larping №1. Check out her collab video with れてんジャダム.

ほのかz : Girl larping №2.

樋野レイ : Girl not larping, talking about life lessons that she learns as a young adult.

ときど : If you like fighting games.
