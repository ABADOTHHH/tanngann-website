The Quiet Art of Reverse Engineering

2025-09-02  //  security

Reverse engineering is less about tools and more about patience. You're having a conversation with code that wasn't written for you, in a language that was never meant to be read.

A disassembler gives you the words. A decompiler gives you a rough translation. Neither gives you understanding — that comes from sitting with it, renaming things, tracing execution paths, and slowly building a mental model of what the author intended.

The best RE work I've done has been unhurried. Come back the next day. The function that looked like noise will suddenly make sense when you've slept on it.
