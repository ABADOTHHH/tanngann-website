My First CTF: Lessons Learned

2025-07-22  //  misc

I went in thinking I'd do well. I did not do well. That's apparently the standard first-CTF experience and it's exactly as humbling as described.

The biggest lesson: read the problem statement three more times before touching a tool. Most of the time I spent stuck was time I spent solving the wrong problem, because I'd misread or assumed.

Second lesson: don't solo the whole thing. The teams that win CTFs are teams. Two people with different backgrounds will see different things in the same challenge. Find your people before the event, not during it.
