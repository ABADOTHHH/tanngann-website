Setting Up a Home Lab on a Budget

2025-10-21  //  projects

You don't need enterprise hardware to run a decent home lab. A few old machines, some open-source software, and a weekend are enough to get something genuinely useful running.

The core of mine is a repurposed mini PC running Proxmox. From there, VMs for pfSense, a logging stack, a few vulnerable-by-design machines for practice, and a NAS built from an old desktop with a ZFS pool.

Total cost was under €200 for everything sourced second-hand. The real investment is time spent configuring it and breaking it until you understand why it broke.
