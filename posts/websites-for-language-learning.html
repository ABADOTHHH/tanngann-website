The hardest part in learning a language, is building a strong immersion environment.

If you can find immersion material in sufficient quantity and quality, you have already won. But this is easier said than done.

Do not underestimate the sheer ammount of time that it may take for you to find enough interesting immersion material to get on track. Go out there, hoard everything you can, and consoom.

General

AJATT (All Japanese All The Time) : Very influential platform in the history of the popularisation of modern language acquisition methods. I find alot of the mindset advice from AJATT still very relevant to my life.

TheMoeWay : Good guide to setup a modern language learning environment.

Animecards : Good introduction to modern language learning tools.

HakuNeko : Download manager with various manga and anime sources preset.

Japanese

nyaa : The best public tracker for otaku related media. Click the "Fap" tab in the header to find the page for mature content (which also includes most VNs).

Chinese

漫畫櫃(manhuagui) : The most complete collection of original and translated comics in Chinese. Download stuff from here via HakuNeko.

量子ACG : The most complete collection of Chinese language VNs.
