Self-Hosting Everything: A Year Later

2025-05-30  //  projects

A year ago I started migrating off cloud services onto hardware I control. Here's what stuck, what I abandoned, and what I'd do differently.

What stuck: Nextcloud for files and calendar, Vaultwarden for passwords, Gitea for private repos, Miniflux for RSS. All of these run happily on modest hardware, have good clients, and have never seriously failed me.

What I abandoned: self-hosted email. It works, technically. But the deliverability headaches, the constant maintenance, and the risk of losing important messages convinced me that some things are better left to specialists. Not every battle is worth fighting.
